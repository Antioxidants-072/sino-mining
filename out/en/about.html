Company Overview

Anhui Sinomining Machinery Co., Ltd. traces its roots back to 1958 and has a registered capital of 110 million RMB. Specializing in the production of mineral processing equipment—including flotation machines, agitation tanks, thickeners, and automatic dosing systems—as well as EPC total contracting for processing plants, it is a large-scale, specialized enterprise integrating R&D, engineering design, manufacturing, installation, commissioning, and operation. The company has long been dedicated to the design, manufacturing, and technical services of mineral processing, metallurgy, chemical, and environmental protection equipment. Anhui Sinomining Machinery is recognized as a National High-Tech Enterprise, an Expert Committee Member of the National Mining Machinery Committee, and a Drafting Unit for 6 National Industry Standards. It has earned prestigious titles including "Outstanding Service Provider in China's Gold Industry," "Provincial Postdoctoral Research Station," "Provincial SRDI Enterprise," "Huaibei Municipal Enterprise Technology Center," and "Huaibei Green Factory." Established in 2008, the company is located in the core area of Suixi Economic Development Zone, Anhui Province. The facility covers an area of 110,000 square meters, housing 60,000 square meters of modern standardized workshops, office facilities, and an R&D center, fully equipped with water, electricity, internet, and road infrastructure. The manufacturing plant currently operates over 160 sets of advanced processing equipment, including machining centers, CNC lathes, shot blasting machines, boring and milling machines, flame cutting machines, plate rolling machines, and submerged arc welding machines. Anhui Sinomining employs over 200 staff members, including more than 60 mid-to-senior level technical personnel.

R&D and Technology

Anhui Sinomining operates a dedicated internal R&D team and scientific research crew, focusing primarily on technical upgrades and manufacturing processes for flotation machines, thickeners, dosing systems, agitation tanks, and their associated automated control systems. The company currently holds over 70 authorized national patents. Building on imported foreign technologies, Anhui Sinomining continuously upgrades its product lines based on real-world field conditions, ensuring both applicability and technological superiority. The company maintains collaborative R&D programs with renowned institutions including Central South University, Northeastern University, China University of Mining and Technology, and University of Science and Technology Beijing. Through these partnerships, Anhui Sinomining has developed advanced technologies—such as dry tailings discharge—that reach leading domestic and international standards.

Quality and After-Sales Service

Anhui Sinomining is certified under ISO9001 Quality Management System, ISO14001 Environmental Management System, and ISO45001 Occupational Health and Safety Management System. The company's core operational philosophy is "Establishing Integrity, Driving Innovation, Delivering Quality Services, and Achieving Customer Satisfaction." Anhui Sinomining operates a comprehensive after-sales service network with localized service hubs across more than 20 provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities in China. Service Commitment: Upon receiving customer inquiries by phone or fax, the service team responds within 10 minutes, provides a problem-solving solution within 1 hour, and arrives on-site within 24 hours to troubleshoot, repair, or replace components. A 24/7 dedicated support hotline (+86-18805856690) is available around the clock.

Track Record and Performance

  • Non-Ferrous Metals: Delivered major national projects including Western Mining's 1.5 Mtpa lead-zinc mine expansion, MCC Group's 2,000 tpd lead-zinc project at an extreme altitude of 4,700 meters, Hunan Linwu Nanfang Mining's 5,000 tpd lead-zinc project, Sichuan Tianqi Lithium, and China Nerin Engineering's copper slag processing. Maintained extensive technical cooperation with China Gold, Chinalco, China Minmetals, Western Mining, Western Gold, and Jinguai Silver
  • Ferrous Metals: Deployed equipment into full commercial production at major mining enterprises such as Chongqing Iron & Steel, Pangang Group, Baosteel Xinjiang Bayi Iron & Steel, Lingang Group, and Hesteel Group
  • Phosphate & Compound Fertilizers: Executed key projects including CNOOC Hubei Dayukou's 6 Mtpa phosphate mine, Yihua Group, Zhongfu Chemical, Hubei Xinyangfeng, and Chongqing Fuling Chemical
  • Non-Metallic Minerals: Built a strong track record across graphite, coal, and quartz processing sectors

Brand and Global Vision

Holding independent import and export rights, Anhui Sinomining exports products to over 20 countries and regions, including Chile, Peru, Russia, the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Zambia, Sudan, Vietnam, the Philippines, Kazakhstan, and Laos. As an Expert Committee Member of the National Mining Machinery Committee, Anhui Sinomining aligns itself with top global brands, pursuing its long-term vision to build an enduring international brand and drive transformation in the global mining industry.
