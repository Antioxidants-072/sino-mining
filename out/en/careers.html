HR Philosophy

Sinomining adheres to the employment philosophy of "people-oriented, right fit for every role, and maximizing personal potential." We provide employees with a strong development platform, strive to inspire their enthusiasm and creativity, and achieve mutual growth between the company and its workforce.

Broad Career Development Space— Sinomining consistently emphasizes the dual cultivation of technical and management talent, configuring personnel from the perspective of overall structure optimization. We establish clear promotion pathways for employees and open up broad horizons for their development.

Recruitment Process

01

Resume Submission

You may submit your resume on-site or via email. Please send your email to a6852178@163.com with the subject line format: "Name - University - Applied Position". We will review and reply within two business days.

02

First Interview

Interview notifications will be sent via telephone. Interviews are held at the company headquarters—this is a two-way process for us to evaluate each other.

03

Written Test

Following the initial interview, candidates may take a written test lasting approximately 30 minutes based on their interview evaluation.

04

Second Interview

Candidates will be notified of a second interview or direct employment offer within 1–2 days based on their written test results.

05

Contract Signing

Candidates who successfully pass both the interview and written test stages will formally sign an employment contract with Sinomining Machinery.

06

Training

Upon signing, Sinomining provides approximately two weeks of free training. Technical personnel and experts from various fields will deliver detailed lectures and hands-on operational training.

07

Probation Period

The probation period ranges from 1 to 3 months. Outstanding performers may be eligible for early full-time confirmation.

08

Onboarding

Upon passing training and meeting probation evaluation criteria, you will officially step into your position as a proud member of Sinomining Machinery.
