Ceramic Filter

Application range

Applied for dewatering of concentrate and tailings in non-ferrous metal, rare metal, ferrous metal and non-metallic mines; dewatering of oxides, electrolytic slag, leaching slag and furnace slag in chemical industry; environmental treatment of sewage, sludge and waste acid. Suitable for materials with fineness from -200 mesh to -450 mesh and various ultra-fine materials

Product advantages

  • Large processing capacity
  • Compact structure, small footprint, convenient installation and maintenance
  • Remarkable technical and economic benefits
Contact our sales experts

Performance features

  1. 1High vacuum degree (0.09-0.098 MPa), low filter-cake moisture
  2. 2Solid content of filtrate is less than 50 ppm. Filtrate can be reused repeatedly to reduce discharge
  3. 3Energy consumption reduced by more than 90% compared with traditional filtration equipment, featuring low energy consumption and low operating cost
  4. 4Filter-cake washing function is added compared with conventional ceramic filters, suitable for materials requiring washing
  5. 5Controlled by PLC, microcomputer and automatic valves, with high automation and reduced labor intensity
  6. 6Compact structure, small footprint, convenient installation and maintenance
  7. 7Advanced liquid-discharge system, suitable for any working conditions

Working principle

Suction zone: Filter plate immersed in slurry adsorbs a layer of filter cake under capillary action combined with vacuum pressure. Filtrate passes through filter plate, enters distribution valve and flows to the discharge tank. Washing zone: Spray washing is applied to the filter cake after it rotates out of the slurry tank. Drying zone: Filter cake continues to be dewatered under high vacuum force. Discharging zone: Scraper automatically discharges the filter cake under non-vacuum condition. Backwashing: Industrial water or filtrate enters ceramic plate through distribution valve and cleans it from inside to outside.
