Reagent Agitation Tank

Application range

Applied for reagent dissolving & preparation, pulp storage and gold leaching in mineral processing plants

Product advantages

  • Used for reagent agitation to achieve uniform mixing
Contact our sales experts

Performance features

  1. 1Models of the same specification consume relatively lower power for energy-saving purpose

Working principle

Driven by the rotating impeller, reagents get dissolved and the liquid inside the tank forms turbulent flow with axial circulating flow. Consequently, slurry and reagents are fully mixed uniformly and discharged through the discharge pipe.
