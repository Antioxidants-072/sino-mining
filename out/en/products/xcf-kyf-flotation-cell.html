XCFⅡ/KYFⅡ-1 ~ XCFⅡ/KYFⅡ-160

XCF‑Ⅱ / KYF‑Ⅱ Type Flotation Machine

Application range

Applicable for roughing, scavenging and cleaning flotation operations of metallic and non-metallic ores

Product advantages

  • Adopts a relatively deep U-shaped cell
  • The impeller blades are backward-inclined at a certain angle, featuring low pressure head, large circulation volume, low power consumption and simple structure
  • A porous cylindrical air distributor is installed inside the impeller chamber to realize uniform air dispersion
  • Options including top-side aeration, automatic oil lubrication and automatic liquid-level control are available
  • Production specification range: 1 m³ – 160 m³
  • Effective volume range: 1 m³ – 160 m³
Contact our sales experts

Performance features

  1. 1Large aeration volume with uniform air dispersion and low power consumption
  2. 2Simple structure for easy maintenance. Wearing parts such as impeller and stator suffer little wear, cutting operating costs by 70–80%
  3. 3Excellent pulp-circulation performance with low reagent consumption, reducing consumption by 10–15%
  4. 4Combined units can be arranged at the same elevation to form a flotation circuit without froth pumps
  5. 5Automatic control devices for pulp level and aeration rate are available with manual/automatic switching and local/centralized control functions

Working principle

Based on the differences in physico-chemical properties of mineral surfaces, valuable minerals are separated by agitation and aeration to obtain target minerals.
