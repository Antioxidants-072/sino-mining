YGC / YFC / YCC / YDC

Mine Car

Application range

Used for transporting bulk materials such as coal, ore and waste rock in mines

Product advantages

  • Strong adaptability to mine roadway track conditions
  • Excellent shock-absorbing performance and high load-bearing capacity
  • Advanced structural technology greatly extends frame service life
  • High reliability with low maintenance cost
Contact our sales experts

Performance features

  1. 1Mine cars can operate individually or in trains according to site conditions, applicable for horizontal roadway transportation, inclined shaft haulage and hoisting via vertical-shaft cage
  2. 2Multiple track gauges available: 600 mm, 762 mm, 900 mm, 1100 mm etc.
  3. 3Divided into five categories by structure and unloading mode: fixed-box, tipping-bucket, single-side curved-rail side-discharge, bottom-(side-) discharge and shuttle mine cars

Working principle

It is a narrow-gauge haulage vehicle for mine material transportation, normally towed by locomotive or winch. Divided into five categories: fixed-box, tipping-bucket, single-side curved-rail side-discharge, bottom-(side-)discharge and shuttle mine cars.
