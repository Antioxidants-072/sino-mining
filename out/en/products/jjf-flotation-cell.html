JJFII-1 ~ JJFII-160

JJF‑II Type Flotation Machine

Application range

Separation of non-ferrous and ferrous metals

Product advantages

  • Shallow-tank design with small-diameter impeller, low rotating speed and low power consumption
  • Large pulp circulation volume
  • Production specification range: 1 m³ – 160 m³
  • Effective volume range: 1 m³ – 160 m³
Contact our sales experts

Performance features

  1. 1Large pulp circulation volume up to 2.5×, favorable for mineralization of pulp, reagents and air
  2. 2Large air intake with good dispersion effect, moderate agitation, good suspension of solid particles, no sand-settling inside cell; no pulp discharging required upon shutdown
  3. 3Shallow impeller submergence depth and low impeller peripheral speed reduce power consumption. It supports a wide treatable particle-size range and delivers high mineral-processing recovery rate

Working principle

Based on the differences in physico-chemical properties of mineral surfaces, valuable minerals are separated by agitation and self-air-suction to obtain target minerals.
