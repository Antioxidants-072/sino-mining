Working principle

The screen plate in the jigging chamber is made of perforated steel plate, woven iron screen mesh or grate bars. Water flows through the screen plate into the jigging chamber to lift the bed layer to a small height and keep it slightly loose. Particles with higher density enter the bottom layer due to greater local pressure and settling velocity, while particles with lower density move to the upper layer. When water flow descends, fine particles with higher density can also enter the lower layer through the gaps of the gradually compacted bed, supplementing density-based stratification. In early designs, a piston was adopted as the mechanism to agitate water flow. The piston chamber was installed beside the jigging chamber, connected at the lower part, and the piston was driven up and down by an eccentric connecting rod mechanism.
