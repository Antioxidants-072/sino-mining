GF-1.1 ~ GF-42

GF‑Type Flotation Machine

Application range

For medium and small-scale enterprises for separation of non-ferrous, ferrous, precious-metal and non-metallic minerals

Product advantages

  • Features dual functions of self-pulp-suction and self-air-suction
  • Large air intake, low energy consumption, high separation efficiency and long service life of wearing parts
  • Production specification range: 1.1 m³ – 42 m³
  • Effective volume range: 1.1 m³ – 42 m³
Contact our sales experts

Performance features

  1. 1Large air intake and low energy consumption
  2. 2Capable of self-air-suction and self-pulp-suction. Adopts horizontal layout and requires no froth pump
  3. 3Low impeller peripheral speed and long service life of wearing parts. The service life of impeller and stator is more than doubled compared with similar flotation machines
  4. 4The clearance between impeller and stator is relatively large. The increase of clearance caused by wear has little influence on air intake capacity
  5. 5The pulp inside the cell realizes vertical double-circulation in a fixed flow pattern, which is favorable for the suspension of coarse-grained minerals

Working principle

Based on the differences in physico-chemical properties of mineral surfaces, valuable minerals are separated by agitation and self-air-suction to obtain target minerals.
