BF-1.2 ~ BF-24

BF flotation cell

Application range

Beneficiation of non-ferrous, ferrous and non-metallic minerals

Product advantages

  • Combines the strengths of existing domestic flotation machine designs
  • Horizontal arrangement with self-returning middlings froth and self-induced air intake
  • Low power consumption, high air intake, low impeller tip speed
  • Available in cell volumes from 1.2 to 24 m³
  • Effective cell volume range of 1.2 to 24 m³
Contact our sales experts

Performance features

  1. 1Impeller built from a closed double-cone body that generates strong pulp recirculation
  2. 2High air intake with low power consumption
  3. 3Each cell combines air intake, pulp suction and flotation in one loop, requiring no auxiliary equipment; horizontal layout makes process changes easy
  4. 4Balanced pulp circulation minimizes coarse sand settling
  5. 5Equipped with automatic pulp level control and electrical control for easy adjustment

Working principle

Based on differences in the physicochemical surface properties of minerals, agitation and self-induced air intake separate the valuable minerals to yield the desired concentrate.
