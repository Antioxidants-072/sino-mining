CTB-618 ~ CTB-1024

Wet‑Type Permanent‑Magnetic Drum Magnetic Separator

Application range

Wet roughing, cleaning and scavenging of strongly magnetic minerals

Product advantages

  • Available in downstream, semi-countercurrent and counter-current types with various drum-surface magnetic field strengths upon customer’s requirements
  • Simple structure, large throughput, convenient operation and easy maintenance
Contact our sales experts

Performance features

  1. 1One single drum can be fitted with three types of tanks to adapt to different separation processes
  2. 2Semi-countercurrent tank: suitable for wet roughing and cleaning of strongly magnetic minerals with particle size 0.5-0 mm, especially for cleaning of 0.15-0 mm fine minerals
  3. 3Counter-current tank: suitable for wet roughing and scavenging of strongly magnetic minerals with particle size 0.6-0 mm, especially for heavy-medium recovery in coal washing plants
  4. 4Downstream tank: suitable for roughing and cleaning of minerals with particle size 6-0 mm
  5. 5Semi-countercurrent tanks and downstream tanks can be used in series to improve concentrate grade
  6. 6The magnetic system is made of high-quality ferrite or composite rare-earth magnetic steel. Average magnetic induction intensity on drum surface ranges from 100 mT to 600 mT
  7. 7Downstream, semi-countercurrent and counter-current types with different surface magnetic-field strengths are available per customer requirements

Working principle

Composite magnetic system consists of high-performance NdFeB rare-earth magnets and ferrite magnets. It features high magnetic-field strength, deep magnetic penetration and low demagnetization risk. Therefore, the equipment delivers large throughput, strong adaptability to production fluctuation and excellent separation performance.
