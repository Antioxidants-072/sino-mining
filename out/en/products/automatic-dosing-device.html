Integrated Combined Dosing Device

Automatic Dosing Device

Application range

Used for treatment of feed water, boiler water, circulating water and wastewater in power plants, and also applicable in petroleum, chemical, environmental protection, water supply systems and other industries

Product advantages

  • Simple structure and convenient installation
  • Easy operation
  • Safe and stable
Contact our sales experts

Performance features

  1. 1It is mainly composed of solution tank, mixer, metering pump, Y-type filter, safety valve, back-pressure valve, check valve, pulse damper, water level gauge, pressure gauge, control cabinet and installation platform, all integrally mounted on one base
  2. 2Users only need to place the combined dosing device in the dosing room, connect the dosing pipes and power supply, then it can be started and put into operation
  3. 3During operation, it automatically detects the pH value of the fluid, intelligently judges and doses the corresponding chemical solution
  4. 4This factory-manufactured complete set of device can greatly reduce the workload of design and on-site construction, basically realizing unattended operation, and providing reliable guarantee for the quality, safety and on-site commissioning of the whole unit
